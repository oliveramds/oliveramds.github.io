I’m Sofia Olivera — With a combined experience of three years, I consistently aim to transfer the knowledge I hold in molecular biology towards other disciplines. I have gained experience in copy editing for scientific and social science open-source journals, as well as for paid online courses. Currently, I have been programming in R, SAS, and Python to support clinical pharmacology data analysis for clinical trials at Pfizer in Manila, Philippines. I have also previously interned at Roche Products Ltd. in Welwyn Garden City, England. Working in a diverse set of teams across multiple time zones, I have strong communication, problem-solving, and organisational skills. Beyond work, I am passionate about science communication and getting those uninterested or afraid of the sciences to understand the value of progressing scientific research in rare diseases.
